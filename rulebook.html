1. General Rules & Regulations

  • No External Electronic Devices: Mobile phones, smartwatches, tablets, USB drives, or secondary laptops are strictly prohibited inside the competition arena.
  • Attendance & Registration: All participants must report to their assigned workstations 15 minutes before the start time for attendance verification.
  • Fair Play: Any form of communication between participants, prompt-copying, or unauthorized internet browsing results in immediate disqualification.

2. Competition Format & Rounds

Round 1: Preliminary Generation & Qualification

  • Written Phase (5 Minutes): Write your detailed image prompt on paper based on the provided theme.
  • Typing & Rendering Phase (5 Minutes): Input your written prompt into the designated AI poster generator to render your entry.
Qualifying Criteria: Top-scoring entries advance based on visual appeal, theme alignment, and composition.

Round 2: Final Advanced Challenge

  • Written Refinement Phase (5 Minutes): Plan a multi-concept prompt scenario on paper.
  • Typing & Rendering Phase (5 Minutes): Generate your final entry in the rendering engine.

3. Submission & Judging Criteria

1. Relevance to Theme

How accurately the generated poster reflects the given competition topic.

2. Creativity & Originality

Unique artistic direction, creative prompt styling, and original concept execution.

3. Visual Quality & Composition

Balance of visual elements, color harmony, text legibility, and rendering precision.

4. Prompt Efficiency

How effectively the written text guides the AI engine without parameter clutter.
